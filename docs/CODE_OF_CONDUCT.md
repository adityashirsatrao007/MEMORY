# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in MEMORY a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **Respectful communication** — critique ideas, not people
- **Constructive feedback** — assume good intent, ask clarifying questions
- **Collaboration first** — help others succeed
- **No harassment** — no trolling, insults, or personal attacks

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to adityashirsatrao007@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
