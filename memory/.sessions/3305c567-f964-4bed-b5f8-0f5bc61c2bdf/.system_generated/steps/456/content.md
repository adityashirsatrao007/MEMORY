<h2 id="what-is">What is scrollytelling?</h2>
<p data-start="1370" data-end="1503">Scrollytelling (a mix of “scrolling” and “storytelling”) is a web design technique where a story unfolds as you move down the page.</p>
<p data-start="1505" data-end="1794">Instead of showing static sections, <strong>scrollytelling uses animations, transitions, and interactive visuals that respond to scrolling.</strong> Text fades in, images shift, charts animate, or videos progress &#8211; all timed with your movement. It’s a bit like directing your own movie just by scrolling.</p>
<p data-start="1796" data-end="1945">Designers love it for <strong>brand campaigns, portfolios, and product launches</strong> because it doesn’t just show what something is &#8211; it shows how it feels<em data-start="1935" data-end="1943">.</em></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h2 id="3D">3D Scrollytelling Examples</h2>
<p>3D scrollytelling brings stories to life through depth, motion, and perspective. These websites use interactive 3D visuals and transitions to draw visitors into fully immersive experiences where each scroll feels like turning a page in a cinematic journey. Ideal for brand storytelling and digital showcases, 3D scrollytelling turns browsing into exploration.</p>
